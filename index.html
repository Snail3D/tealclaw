Your AI agent,
in your pocket.

TealClaw is the iPhone app for your Hermes agent. Hold a claw, say the words, and your agent is on it — from anywhere.

Set it up — 5 minutes How it works

Runs on your own fleet. Your agent, your hardware, your data.

🦞

Hold-to-talk

Hold the claw and speak — words appear live as you talk. Release to typewrite them into the field, swipe up to fire straight at your agent. Double-tap to latch a constant listen.

📸

Show it things

Shoot a photo or pick from the library and ask about it. Your agent sees it natively — reading labels, checking wiring, judging the fridge situation.

🎮

AI-built apps & games

Ask for a game or a tool and it's built on the spot — shipped into your chat as a tappable card. Pomodoro timers, tap games, converters, mini-dashboards.

✅

Real approvals

When your agent needs permission for something spicy, buttons show up in the chat — Approve, This Session, Always, Deny. One tap and it keeps working.

🔔

Push + home channel

Replies buzz your phone when the app is closed. Cron jobs and deliverables route to your pocket automatically — the app is your agent's home base.

🗨️

GIF reactions

Your agent talks like a person and reacts like one too — topical GIFs pop up as floating, draggable stickers you can flick away.

Three steps to a pocket agent

1

Run the gateway

Start your Hermes agent's tealclaw profile — it exposes a tiny bridge on your machine.

# on your Mac
hermes --profile tealclaw gateway
2

Point the app at it

Paste your bridge URL and token. One tap on Test connection and you know you're live.

3

Hold the claw

Talk to your agent like it's a person — because it basically is. Photos, games, approvals, push. Done.
